FAQ

Things people usually ask.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A brand identity typically takes 4–6 weeks. A full product design engagement runs 8–16 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before anything starts.

Do you build Shopify stores, or just design them?

Both. We handle everything from UX and visual design to development and launch, so you work with one team from start to finish.

Do you work with startups or only established companies?

Both. We've worked with early-stage startups and large enterprises. What matters is that you have a real problem and are serious about solving it well.

What's your minimum engagement?

We typically don't take on projects under 3 weeks. Good design takes time, and we'd rather do fewer things well.

How do we get started?

Tell us what you're building, your timeline, and your budget range. We'll reply within 48 hours to set up a call.

About

Clear, usable, market‑ready design.

Born in Shenzhen in 2021. Based in Toronto since 2024.

01 · Shenzhen, 2021

It started in a city that makes things.

Zepto Lab began as two designers in Shenzhen, where so much of the world's products are made. There, a sketch only matters if it can be built. We learned to design with production in mind: materials, costs, timelines, and the person who will one day hold the thing.

02 · Along the way

One project led to the next.

A product needed packaging. The packaging needed a brand. The brand needed a place to sell. Following that thread took us across tech, consumer products, real estate, lifestyle, fashion, and entertainment, and to 20+ companies, from early-stage startups to OPPO, Vanke, and Tsingtao.

03 · Toronto, 2024

Then we brought the studio to where we learned design.

Every one of us has lived in North America for more than ten years, so this market isn't something we learned from research. We know how customers here shop, what they trust, and what makes a brand feel local. With roots on both sides, we help Chinese companies launch in North America, and North American brands find their footing in China.

04 · Today

Small team, whole picture.

We focus on UI/UX, branding, and marketing. For smaller teams, we're the partner who understands the technology, the product, and the business, so the work doesn't stop at looking good. It gets built, launched, and sold.
